MY FRIENDS ♡

To my dear chengaayees,

Sometimes I look at our photos and wonder how we got so lucky to find each other.

We have the most random conversations, laugh at things nobody else would understand, make plans we may or may not follow through on, and somehow turn ordinary days into stories worth keeping.

Life will get busy. There'll be new places, new people, different routines, and days when getting everyone together feels impossible. But I hope we never stop trying.

No matter where life takes us, a piece of my best days will always have you in it.

Here's to us, always. ♡
